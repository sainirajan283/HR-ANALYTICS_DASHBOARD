## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an **HR Analytics Dashboard** built in **Microsoft Power BI**, designed to help HR teams and management gain data-driven insights into workforce trends, employee attrition, and organizational performance.

The dashboard transforms raw HR data into interactive, visual reports — enabling faster decision-making around employee retention, department-wise headcount, salary distribution, and attrition patterns.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee **attrition rate** and identify key contributing factors
- Visualize **department-wise** and **job-role-wise** employee distribution
- Track **salary slabs**, **age groups**, and **years at company** vs attrition
- Provide **gender-based** and **education-based** workforce breakdowns
- Enable HR teams to make **proactive retention decisions**

## 🖥️ Dashboard Features

|---|---|

## 💡 Insights Derived

- Employees in the **26–35 age group** show the highest attrition
- **Sales department** records the highest attrition rate among all departments
- Employees earning **below ₹5K/month** are most likely to leave
- **Life Sciences & Medical** educational backgrounds dominate the workforce
- Attrition is highest in **the first year** of employment
